### 2. The "Private Hospitals Group" Package

**Best For: Growing Practices in Competitive Areas**

If you have an established practice but find yourself in a "vet-heavy" city where the competition for the first page of Google is fierce, this is the package for you. To win in high-competition markets, you need more than just a good website; you need to become the local authority. PAW Rank specializes in helping practices achieve this.

- **Everything in the Basic Package: **Plus a much more aggressive strategy.
- **2 Multiple Locations: **Hospitals with multiple branches are also covered in this package but you need to pay for each location seperately.
- **Authority Building: **We produce high-quality, helpful content that answers pet owners' most pressing questions, positioning you as the expert in your region.
- **Backlink Outreach: **We actively reach out to other websites to earn "backlinks," which act as votes of confidence in the eyes of Google, helping you leapfrog over competitors.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO): **Getting people to your website is only half the battle.CRO is the process of optimizing your site to ensure those visitors actually pick up the phone or book an appointment.

**
Why is CRO important? Without it, you are essentially "pouring water into a leaky bucket." We analyze user behavior to make sure your website turns "lookers" into "booked appointments."
