## Questions You Should Ask Before Paying an SEO Agency

You don’t need to understand SEO well enough to judge every technical decision.
But before paying an agency month after month, you should understand **what you’re paying for, how the work will be measured, what you should realistically expect and what happens if the relationship ends.**
Here are straightforward answers to the questions veterinary practice owners commonly have before investing in SEO.

Why do SEO prices vary so much between agencies?
+

Because the word “SEO” can describe very different amounts of work.

One provider may primarily send you keyword reports and make occasional website changes. Another may be researching your market, improving service pages, working on your Google Business Profile, fixing technical issues, improving conversion paths, building authority, tracking enquiries and continuously deciding what should be worked on next.

So when comparing prices, don’t compare the monthly number alone. Compare:

- What work is actually included

- How much work can be completed each month

- Who is doing the work

- Whether the strategy is customized to your practice

- How results are measured

- Whether the agency can show previous work and results

- How clearly they can explain what they are doing

The cheapest SEO is not automatically the best value. And the most expensive SEO is not automatically the best either. The question is whether the work you’re paying for makes sense for your practice and your goals.

Why is SEO a monthly service instead of a one-time project?
+

Some parts of SEO can be completed once. An audit, initial research or fixing a specific technical problem are good examples.

But an ongoing SEO campaign involves more than fixing a checklist once. Month by month, we may be:

- Developing or improving important service pages

- Strengthening your local Google presence

- Fixing technical issues

- Monitoring search performance

- Reviewing competitors

- Improving the paths visitors use to contact your practice

- Tracking calls and forms where possible

- Identifying new opportunities

- Deciding what should be prioritized next

Your monthly payment funds the ongoing implementation, improvement, measurement and strategy required by your plan.

How long does SEO take before we see results?
+

SEO should be treated as a multi-month investment rather than something that switches on overnight.

How quickly a practice improves depends on factors such as:

- Where your website is starting from

- How competitive your local market is

- How strong competing practices already are

- The services you want to grow

- The condition of your website

- Your existing Google presence and reputation

- How much campaign capacity your plan provides

Some improvements may become visible earlier than others. We would rather give you expectations based on your actual starting position than promise the same timeline to every veterinary practice.

How will I know what you are actually doing every month?
+

You shouldn’t have to guess. Your reporting should help you understand both: What happened and What we worked on. Depending on your plan and available tracking, that can include:

- Work completed during the month

- Pages created or improved

- Changes in important search visibility

- Google Business Profile performance

- Calls and forms where tracking allows

- Opportunities or issues we have identified

- What we recommend prioritizing next

If something in your report doesn’t make sense, we should be able to explain it without hiding behind SEO terminology.

What if I don’t understand SEO reports?
+

That is completely fine. You are running a veterinary practice. You should not have to become an SEO specialist just to understand whether your marketing investment is being managed properly.

Instead of expecting you to interpret dozens of technical metrics, we focus on explaining questions such as:

- Are more relevant people finding the practice?

- Which services are becoming more visible?

- Are people calling or submitting forms where we can track them?

- What did we work on?

- What should we focus on next?

How do you measure whether SEO is actually working?
+

We look at more than rankings alone. Depending on what can be tracked for your practice, we may look at:

- Visibility for important veterinary searches

- Performance of priority service pages

- Google Business Profile activity

- Organic website traffic

- Calls from website visitors

- Appointment-request actions

- Which services or pages appear to be creating enquiries

Rankings and traffic help us understand what is happening. But where tracking allows, we want to go further and understand whether that visibility is producing real opportunities for your practice.

Can you guarantee that we will rank #1 or get a certain number of appointments?
+

No. We do not guarantee a specific Google position, number of calls or number of booked appointments.

There are too many factors outside the control of any SEO agency, including local demand, competition, Google’s search systems, your reputation, your availability, pricing, practice capacity and what happens after someone contacts your team.

What we can be accountable for is the work we agree to perform, the strategy behind it, the reporting we provide and the decisions we make using the information available. We would rather show you evidence than sell you a guarantee we cannot responsibly control.

You have every right to ask what is being done to your website and online presence. Our approach is built around work we can explain to you, including things such as:
