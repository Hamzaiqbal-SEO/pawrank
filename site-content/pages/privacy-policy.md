# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025  ·  Effective Immediately

---

Welcome to **Paw Rank** ("we," "us," or "our"). We provide professional SEO services exclusively for veterinary clinics and veterinarians worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit **pawrank.com** or engage with our services.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our website.

## Information We Collect:

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out our contact form, request a consultation, or communicate with us directly. The types of data we collect include:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Clinic or practice name
- Business location & website URL
- Service enquiry details

We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This is collected through cookies and analytics tools described below.

## How We Use Your Information:

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **To respond to enquiries** — When you contact us via our form, we use your details to get back to you promptly.
- **To deliver our services** — Your business information helps us tailor our SEO strategy specifically for your veterinary practice.
- **To improve our website** — Analytics data helps us understand how visitors use Paw Rank and where we can improve.
- **To send relevant communications** — With your consent, we may send you updates, tips, or offers related to veterinary SEO.
- **To comply with legal obligations** — We may use or retain information as required by applicable law.

## Marketing Communications:

We plan to send occasional email communications to clients and subscribers, including SEO tips, industry updates, and service announcements relevant to veterinary practices.

You will only receive marketing emails if you have explicitly opted in — either through a sign-up form on our website or by requesting to be added to our list. Every marketing email we send will include a clear and easy **unsubscribe option**. You can opt out at any time with no questions asked.

## Data Storage & Security:

Paw Rank is based in Pakistan and serves clients globally. Your data may be stored and processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence, including through our third-party tool providers whose servers may be located in the United States, European Union, or elsewhere.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are typically retained for up to 2 years.

---

## Your Rights:

Regardless of where you are located, you have the following rights over your personal data:

- **Right to Access** — You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification** — You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Right to Erasure** — You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
- **Right to Restriction** — You can ask us to limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
- **Right to Object** — You can object to our processing of your data for marketing purposes at any time.
- **Right to Data Portability** — You can request your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy:

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
